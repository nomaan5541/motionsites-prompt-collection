## 1. Executive Summary & Legal Posture

The **MotionSites Prompts Collection** is an open-source, community-driven research and educational initiative. The repository archives natural-language AI prompts, layout structural tokens, color palettes, and UI component specifications intended to facilitate AI-assisted web development and human-AI collaborative design.

This project operates in full good-faith compliance with the safe harbor provisions of Section 512 of Title 17 of the United States Code (the "DMCA"). We respect the legitimate intellectual property rights of all content creators, visual designers, software engineers, and digital artists.

## 2. Nature of Repository Content

All materials hosted within this repository consist of:
1. **Natural Language Prompts**: Transformative textual descriptions, layout specifications, and prompt instructions designed for interpretation by Large Language Models (LLMs).
2. **Metadata & JSON Schema**: Technical parameters including color tokens, typography stacks, aspect ratios, and categorization tags.
3. **Public Visual References**: Low-resolution thumbnails, texture captures, and video loops referenced for identification, demonstration, and transformative review under fair use doctrines.

This repository does **not** host, distribute, or execute proprietary closed-source server applications, private API keys, user credentials, or circumvention tools.

## 3. Statutory Requirements for Notice of Alleged Infringement

Pursuant to **17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)(A)**, to be legally effective, any formal Notification of Claimed Infringement must be provided in writing to our designated contact and must include substantially all of the following elements:

1. **Physical or Electronic Signature**:
   A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

2. **Identification of Copyrighted Work**:
   Clear identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or, if multiple copyrighted works at a single online site are covered by a single notification, a representative list of such works (including registration numbers or direct authoritative URLs where available).

3. **Identification of Allegedly Infringing Material**:
   Specific identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing or to be the subject of infringing activity, including exact repository file paths, prompt slugs, folder names, or URLs within this repository sufficient to permit the repository maintainer to locate the material immediately.

4. **Claimant Contact Information**:
   Information reasonably sufficient to permit the repository maintainer to contact the complaining party, including:
   - Full Legal Name
   - Title and Corporate/Organizational Affiliation
   - Physical Mailing Address
   - Primary Telephone Number
   - Valid Electronic Mail Address

5. **Good Faith Statement**:
   A statement that the complaining party has a good-faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.

6. **Accuracy and Perjury Statement**:
   A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that the complaining party is authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

## 4. Verification & Action Workflow

Upon receipt of a formal notification satisfying all statutory criteria of 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3):

1. **Initial Assessment**: The repository maintainer will promptly review the notice to verify that all statutory elements are present and identify the exact assets referenced.
2. **Expeditious Removal or Disabling**: Access to the allegedly infringing file, prompt text, metadata record, or media preview will be disabled or permanently removed from the repository.
3. **Catalog & Database Resynchronization**: The target item will be purged from all auto-generated TypeScript catalogs (`src/data/prompts.generated.ts`) and JSON datasets (`motionsites_all_prompts.json`, `preview_mapping.json`) during the immediate subsequent build cycle.
4. **Notice to Contributor**: If the material was submitted by a third-party contributor, reasonable steps will be taken to forward a copy of the notification to the contributor.

To be effective, a Counter-Notification must include:
1. The physical or electronic signature of the subscriber.
2. Identification of the material that has been removed or to which access has been disabled and the location at which the material appeared before it was removed or disabled.
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that the subscriber has a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification of the material to be removed or disabled.
4. The subscriber's name, address, and telephone number, and a statement that the subscriber consents to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district in which the address is located, or if outside the United States, for any judicial district in which GitHub or the service provider may be found, and that the subscriber will accept service of process from the person who provided notification under subsection (c)(1)(C) or an agent of such person.
